## Limits

No model-requested action was executed. A relevant deferred query is not a completed lookup; a valid JSON report is not a correct answer. The 17 cases × two repeats are descriptive paired observations, not 68 independent completed tasks. C/L differ in both lexical fields and length, while L retains query text, ranking scores/order and shared guidance; this cannot isolate a causal lexical-label effect. The fixture's scripted upstream context also need not reproduce actual live routing.

No aggregate semantic pass count, statistical claim, promotion decision or implied full100 gain is provided. Reviewer differences in strict finality or qualifier-loss flags should remain visible rather than being automatically merged.
