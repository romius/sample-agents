Builds on codex-sol61 d4922r1/d4946r1/d4947r1 and exact last-model inputs d4911r1. All originals are retained under upstream/. Exact archived record worlds independently contain101 products each. The Metabo standard-head query gives FLAT a lexical lead because head occurs in flat-head; standard is absent from every sibling. A rank lead is not uniqueness. Both DeWalt kits include a case; forcing one is unjustified.

bounded_lookup_evidence preserves original matches/order/scores and attaches same-family siblings, lexical coverage, and explicit incomplete/truncated flags. The caller must supply a verified sku_lookup scope; unrelated scopes are unchanged. No actual runtime intent/router hook exists. The parent index does not prove completeness, even where the archived fixture is independently complete. Missing words are not negative properties. Family priority follows displayed matches so unrelated families cannot consume the entire evidence budget first.

Results are observation-construction checks only: no model call, answer rewrite or outcome improvement demonstrated.
